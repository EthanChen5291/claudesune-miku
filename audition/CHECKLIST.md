# Library audition checklist

Every entry here is `ratified: false` (or equivalent — see DECISIONS D21/D23.3): hand-written by the building LLM, never auditioned. Mark each KEEP or KILL by ear; nothing is used again in a real song until this is done.

Open each listen.html, hit play, and follow along — sections play back-to-back in the order below, one entry at a time (only one label/section is ever sounding at once, so no muting needed unless you want to skip ahead).

## rhythms-4-4

`audition/rhythms-4-4/listen.html` — 4/4, 118bpm, key F:minor

- [ ] **four_floor** (as drums, sound bd) — cycles 0–4
      The floor. Beat 3 slightly heavier than 2/4 so the bar breathes instead of marching.
- [ ] **offbeat_8ths** (as notes) — cycles 4–8
      Pure offbeat pulse — the exhale between four-floor kicks. Accent tilts to the 3& lift.
- [ ] **backbeat_ghost** (as drums, sound sd) — cycles 8–12
      Backbeat 2 & 4 with ghost 16ths dragging into the next beat — the ghosts ARE the groove.
- [ ] **tresillo** (as drums, sound bd) — cycles 12–16
      3-in-8, the most exported rhythm on earth. Middle onset softest so the last one pushes forward.
- [ ] **son_clave_3** (as drums, sound rim) — cycles 16–20
      One-bar son clave (3+2 compressed). Works as melodic rhythm as well as percussion.
- [ ] **dembow** (as drums, sound cp) — cycles 20–24
      Dembow skeleton — boom-ch-boom-chick with a soft pickup into the next bar.
- [ ] **sixteenth_drive** (as drums, sound hh) — cycles 24–28
      Continuous 16ths with a beat-weighted accent hierarchy — drive without flatness. The last two lift into the next bar.
- [ ] **swung_lofi_hats** (as drums, sound hh) — cycles 28–32
      Straight 8ths swung hard (0.55). Accents lean on 1 and 3 so the swing reads as lazy, not lopsided.
- [ ] **lazy_dilla** (as drums, sound hh) — cycles 32–36
      Dilla drag: every offbeat lands late by an uneven hair (1/64 vs 1/48) — drunk but deliberate.
- [ ] **two_step_kick** (as drums, sound bd) — cycles 36–40
      UKG two-step kick — hole on beat 2 is the point; the 3& shoves the bar forward.
- [ ] **anticipation_bass** (as drums, sound bd) — cycles 40–44
      Pre-empts beats 2 and 1 by a 16th — the push-pull against a straight kick. Audition r1: too sparse-with-holes to carry a note line; demoted to percussion.
- [ ] **push_pull_16s** (as drums, sound rim) — cycles 44–48
      The §2 handoff rhythm: strong anchors on 1 and 4 with syncopated pushes between — verified ground truth. Audition r1: reads as percussion, not a note-carrier.
- [ ] **gallop_arp** (as drums, sound rim) — cycles 48–52
      Trance gallop (dum-da-da ×4). Accents on the long note. Audition r1: works as percussion, not as a note-filler; demoted.
- [ ] **sparse_pedal** (as bass line) — cycles 52–56
      Two anchors per bar. For breakdowns and bridges — space is the material.
- [ ] **even_8ths** (as notes) — cycles 56–60
      Straight 8ths with a beat-weighted accent tilt. The neutral note-carrier — when the CONTOUR is the story, this stays out of its way.
- [ ] **pushed_comp_2bar** (as comp, 2 bars) — cycles 60–64
      Ethan's 2-bar push comp: bar 1 states 1-2-3 on the beat with light low bounces on 2&, 3&, 4; bar 2 anticipates on the &s of 1 and 2, then lands 3-4. The classic anticipation/"rhythmic push" comping family.
- [ ] **charleston_2bar** (as comp, 2 bars) — cycles 64–68
      Charleston (1, 2&) asked and answered: bar 2 repeats it and adds a soft low pickup on 4 leading back to the top.
- [ ] **bossa_comp_2bar** (as comp, 2 bars) — cycles 68–72
      Bossa comp skeleton over two bars: bar 1 pushes 1, 2&, 4; bar 2 answers on 2 and 3& — the offbeat half of the phrase.
- [ ] **call_response_2bar** (as notes, 2 bars) — cycles 72–76
      Two-bar phrase logic for a line: busy call in bar 1 (five notes leaning on 1 and 3), sparse answer in bar 2 (three long notes). Eight onsets — 8-degree contours like question_answer land exactly.

## contours

`audition/contours/listen.html` — 4/4, 112bpm, key C:dorian

- [ ] **arch_classic** (shape arch) — cycles 0–2
      The textbook arch — up to the 6th degree and home again. Safe, singable, resolves itself. (Audition r1 heard it smeared by the old cycling realization; the data was always the arch.)
- [ ] **rise_anthem** (shape rise) — cycles 2–4
      Straight climb to the octave. Wants a chorus; pair with dense rhythm for lift.
- [ ] **fall_sigh** (shape fall) — cycles 4–6
      Descending sigh from the octave through a FLAT 6th (Ethan: "flat the second note") — the darkened submediant is the sigh. In modes already carrying a b6 the alteration clamps off.
- [ ] **zigzag_narrow** (shape zigzag) — cycles 6–8
      Two steps up, one back — motion that never leaves the pocket. Good under a vocal-like lead.
- [ ] **pendulum_fifth** (shape oscillation) — cycles 8–10
      Root–fifth pendulum with a 3rd-degree passing exit. Bassline-shaped but works an octave up.
- [ ] **spiral_up** (shape rise) — cycles 10–12
      Climb with shrinking backsteps (-2, then -1s) that lands ON the octave — the old data never gained real ground and got killed in audition r1. Tension climber with a summit.
- [ ] **hook_drop** (shape peak-fall) — cycles 12–14
      The hook IS the repeated top note (octave ×3), then the drop: down to the 5th, a turn, and a stepwise tumble home. Audition r1 killed the old arpeggio-shaped version.
- [ ] **valley** (shape valley) — cycles 14–16
      Inverse arch — descends into the root, sits, climbs out. The held root in the middle is the point.
- [ ] **bass_root_five** (shape static) — cycles 16–18
      Root-root-fifth-root. The most boring line in music, which is why it works under everything.
- [ ] **dorian_lift** (shape arch) — cycles 18–20
      Arch that peaks on degree 5 — the actual 6th scale degree, dorian's raised 6th, the one note that names the mode. (Old data was off by one and peaked on the 7th; audition r1 caught it.)
- [ ] **minimal_dyad** (shape oscillation) — cycles 20–22
      Root–fourth-degree seesaw. Hypnotic; for breakdowns and lo-fi where less is the material.
- [ ] **question_answer** (shape arch) — cycles 22–24
      Two four-note halves: the first ends open (on the 3rd), the second answers home. Phrase logic built in.

## voicings

`audition/voicings/listen.html` — 4/4, 100bpm, key C:minor

- [ ] **closed_stack** — cycles 0–4
      Plain close position. The reference voicing — use when the pad should disappear into the mix.
- [ ] **drop2** — cycles 4–8
      True drop-2: the FIFTH (second-from-top of the close core) dropped an octave — jazz warmth, solid bass note. Audition r1 caught the old shapes dropping the 7th, which put the leading tone / b7 a step under the root.
- [ ] **shell_37** — cycles 8–12
      Rootless 3-and-7 shells (+9 on ninths). Lo-fi Rhodes language — the bass owns the root, stay out of its way.
- [ ] **spread_tenth** — cycles 12–16
      Root, 7th/5th, 10th — wide open spread. Cinematic pads; leaves a canyon for the lead.
- [ ] **cluster_upper** — cycles 16–20
      Upper-structure cluster (5-7-9 region), no root, seconds allowed to rub. Dreamy; needs reverb.

## transitions

`audition/transitions/listen.html` — 4/4, 122bpm, key F:minor

- [ ] **riser_hat_swell** (riser, *→chorus) — tail of the section before chorus_riser (cycles 2–4)
      Hat roll that opens up — gain and filter climb together over the approach. The polite lift.
- [ ] **riser_noise_sweep** (riser, breakdown→chorus) — tail of the section before chorus_noise (cycles 8–12)
      The EDM noise riser: four bars of white noise swelling out of a breakdown. Shameless, effective.
- [ ] **fill_snare_roll** (fill, *→chorus) — tail of the section before chorus_fill (cycles 19–20)
      One-bar 16th snare roll, strict crescendo. Says "here it comes" in every genre since disco.
- [ ] **fill_tom_run** (fill, *→*) — tail of the section before any_tom (cycles 27–28)
      Descending tom run ending on doubled snares — the acoustic-kit answer to the snare roll.
- [ ] **impact_boom** (impact, *→*) — head of any_impact (cycles 36–37)
      Downbeat punctuation: pitched-down kick boom plus a splashy open hat. Lands the arrival.
- [ ] **sweep_downlifter** (sweep, chorus→*) — head of after_sweep (cycles 44–45)
      Energy exhaust after a chorus — everything falls (gain, filter) across the first bar of the calmer section.
- [ ] **sub_drop** (impact, breakdown→chorus) — head of chorus_drop (cycles 52–53)
      Sub boom on the drop downbeat. Felt more than heard; the reward for the withheld low end.

## interlocks-4-4

`audition/interlocks-4-4/listen.html` — 4/4, 118bpm, key F:minor

- [ ] **house_pump** (four_floor + offbeat_8ths) — cycles 0–4
      Kick on the floor, bass breathing in every gap — 100% complementary. The house engine.
- [ ] **two_step_snap** (two_step_kick + backbeat_ghost) — cycles 4–8
      UKG kick holes land exactly where the backbeat and its ghosts live. The ghosts drag, the kick shoves.
- [ ] **clave_push** (tresillo + anticipation_bass) — cycles 8–12
      Tresillo kick with a bass that anticipates the beats the kick skips. Shared downbeat is the anchor — everything else interlocks.
- [ ] **dembow_ride** (dembow + sixteenth_drive) — cycles 12–16
      Dembow skeleton under continuous accented 16ths — the hats supply the grid the dembow syncopates against. Coincidences intended.
