# Vibes census — every vibe/mood vocabulary in motif-engine and how they connect

Compiled 2026-08-25 from the working tree (branch `main`). All observations below are
facts about what the files SAY; any suggestion of how vocabularies *should* connect is a
CANDIDATE only — nothing here is ear-ratified, and per A6.1 the owner's ear is the only
quality signal in this project.

## 2. ldrolez progression mood tags — `src/lib/progressions.js`

**22 distinct Capitalized tags** on 188 entries (pack `ldrolez`), generated by
`scripts/import-ldrolez.mjs` from `vendor/ldrolez/chords.py` ("the SOURCE's
human-authored tags and are real data"). Verbatim, with entry counts:

Mysterious 59 · Nostalgic 57 · Hopeful 53 · Triumphant 33 · Romantic 31 · Rebellious 18 ·
Joyful 16 · Surprised 15 · Sad 13 · Dark 13 · Tender 9 · Playful 7 · Spiritual 6 ·
Cadence 6 · Relaxed 5 · Peaceful 4 · Empowered 4 · Lonely 2 · Fearful 2 · Excited 2 ·
Dramatic 2 · Anguished 1

Notes:
- There is **no "Happy" tag** in the actual set (the source uses Joyful); "Dramatic"
  and "Peaceful" exist but are rare and one of each came from a D60 ear-correction.
- `Cadence` is a **structural** marker living inside the mood array (dupes merged by the
  importer so the mark is never dropped).
- **D60 ear-corrections** (in `import-ldrolez.mjs` `MOOD_CORRECTIONS`, so regeneration
  reproduces them): `mod_vi_bVIM_bVIIM_I` drop Triumphant → add Dramatic ("a bit darker
  than triumphant"); `mod_vi_IV_I_IIM` drop Peaceful → add Triumphant ("more triumphant
  than peaceful"). Ruling: where the ear disagreed in so many words, the correction
  outranks the tag.

**Consumed by:** `findProgressions({ moods })` (any-of, case-insensitive — defined in
`scripts/progressions-tail.js`, appended into progressions.js) and the audition page
mood-filter dropdown + search (`scripts/audition-progressions.mjs` lines 278/456/463).
**Never consumed by arrange.js** — instrument casting never sees progression tags.

## 5. Video-pack vibe words — `src/lib/progressions-videos.js`

**34 distinct lowercase words** on 19 mood-bearing entries (hand-written, D57). This is
a *different register* from every other vocabulary — mostly **motion and texture**
language, not emotion:

- motion/shape: suspended (3), unresolved (2), chromatic (2), circling, floating,
  drifting, falling, sinking, rolling, turning, cycling, pivoting, lifting, descending,
  walking-down, resolving
- texture/idiom: lush, plush, rich, smooth, groovy, soulful, urbane, radiant, assured,
  classic, modal, yearning
- shared with the instrument vocabulary (6 words): warm, tender, gentle, dark, bright,
  hopeful

`video-corpus.md` itself contains **no mood/vibe vocabulary** — it is technique language
(9sus4 as structure, altered dominants as the marked event, tritone-adjacent slides,
layering grammars). Ethan's endorsement is of the SOURCE ("i am a fan of how all the
songs sound... if prompted for the appropriate context and style"), recorded as
`sourceEndorsed: true`; ratified stays false on every entry.

## 8. The atlas — `scripts/build-atlas.mjs` → `src/lib/atlas.js` (+ `src/lib/undertale-context.js`)

The atlas keys on **computed features and curated context, never on mood words**, across
three axes (D35; 425 progressions / 95 figures / 84 rhythms):

- **vibe axis** (progressions only; `vibeFeatures()`): family; borrowed-degree `colors`
  (bII bIII bV bVI bVII II III VI VII IV V / #I #II #IV #V #VI) + colorCount; minorish;
  seventh; dim; sus; harmonicRhythm; rootMotion {fifths, step, third, same, tritone};
  cadence ∈ {authentic, plagal, resolves, half, loop}; tension curve (circle-of-fifths
  distance per chord) + tensionShape ∈ {flat, arch, rising, falling}. "Grounded in the
  harmony-emotion literature" (D35) — but the grounding is numeric, no words.
- **speed axis**: onsets/density/subdivision/syncopation/legato/bpm, plus Levenshtein
  over 48-step onset strings.
- **context axis** (the one axis "no computation can produce", HAND-CURATED in
  undertale-context.js): `role` (controlled vocab §6), `character`, `motifs` (leitmotif
  family slugs — "real similarity ground truth, not a vibe"), `arc` ∈ {early, mid, late,
  climax, ending}. contextDist: same song 0.05 < shared motif 0.25 < same character
  0.35 < same role 0.6 < 1.
- Section labels are computed strings like `"major · diatonic · ~1 chord/bar"` or
  `"major · bII · ~1 chord/bar"` — features again, not vibes.
- Runtime: `neighborhood()` (returns NAMES plural — anti-copy-paste ruling),
  `sectionOf()`, seeded `pickFrom()`.

## Overlaps and mismatches

- **M1 — The only wired bridge is role/family → instrument.** ROLE_MOODS ∪ FAMILY_MOODS
  = 40 words, all 40 present in the instrument vocabulary (by construction, post-D46).
  Instrument-side residue: `ethereal` matches nothing.
- **M2 — Progression tags and instrument moods never meet in code.** 9 words overlap
  case-insensitively (nostalgic, hopeful, triumphant, sad, dark, tender, playful,
  relaxed, lonely) but no code path joins them: findProgressions lowercases for its own
  filter only, and arrange.js's `s.moods` comes from `atlas.context?.moods` — a field
  undertale-context.js does not define, so it is effectively always null. 13 ldrolez
  tags (Mysterious, Romantic, Rebellious, Joyful, Surprised, Spiritual, Cadence,
  Peaceful, Empowered, Fearful, Excited, Dramatic, Anguished) have no instrument-side
  counterpart at all.
- **M3 — Casing split**: ldrolez/unison tags are Capitalized; instrument, role-table, and
  video words are lowercase. Retrieval papers over it per-call; nothing normalizes at
  the data layer.
- **M4 — The video pack speaks a third register** (motion/texture: circling, plush,
  walking-down) overlapping the instrument vocabulary on only 6 words; no consumer
  matches on these words today.
- **M5 — 251 entries (vgmusic + undertale) carry no vibe words**; their only handles are
  computed atlas features and context roles.
- **M6 — Stale cross-reference**: instruments.js calls its moods "atlas vibe words it
  belongs with", but the atlas's vibe axis is numeric features — there is no word
  vocabulary in the atlas for an instrument to "belong with."
- **M7 — 'Cadence' is structure filed as mood** (kept deliberately by the importer's
  merge rule, but it means the mood array is not purely emotive).

## The gap the owner named

Every WORD vocabulary in the repo is an **emotion/character adjective set** (sad,
hopeful, warm, eerie, Mysterious, Triumphant...) — including the video pack's
motion/texture words, which describe the harmony's behavior, not a place or a doing.
The single environment-shaped vocabulary is the 13-role game-scene list
(boss/shop/town/...), and it is **closed and non-compositional**: each role carries a
FIXED emotion list (ROLE_MOODS), so there is no way to express:

- **emotion × environment** — "happy shop" vs. gloomy shop: `shop` always pulls
  playful/folk/retro/jazzy/quirky; the emotion half cannot be varied independently;
- **an ENVIRONMENT that isn't a game-menu scene** — "construction" (a construction
  site/work setting) has no role, no tag, no feature;
- **an ACTIVITY as its own dimension** — "fight activity" as distinct from the `battle`
  role's baked-in mood list and energy contour.

In short: the engine can say how music FEELS (many overlapping emotion vocabularies) and
how it BEHAVES (atlas features, archetype energy), and can name 13 canonical game
scenes — but it has no vocabulary for WHERE the music is or WHAT is being done, and no
way to compose an emotion with a place/activity. (The exact phrases "happy shop",
"construction", "fight activity" appear in the owner's brief, not in any repo file; the
closest in-repo hooks are the fixed ROLE_MOODS table, the D57 endorsement clause
"if prompted for the appropriate context and style", and the MOOD CALIBRATION note
anticipating a prompt→params vibe-word layer.)

**CANDIDATE (unratified, for the owner's ear/decision only):** any future
environment/activity axis would slot most naturally beside `role` in the context block
(undertale-context.js pattern: curated, sourced, never invented) and into the
prompt→params layer the MOOD CALIBRATION ruling already anticipates — but which words,
and whether they earn a place, is the ear's call.

## Consumption map (one line each)

- instruments.js `moods` → arrange.js casting score (`moodHit*1.2`).
- progressions.js/unison/videos `moods` → `findProgressions({moods})` + audition filter UI.
- vgmusic/undertale `moods: []` → nothing.
- ROLE_SLATE / ROLE_MOODS / ROLE_FORM (13 roles) → arrange.js slate pick, mood list, form shape; role sourced from undertale-context.js via audition-undertale.mjs.
- FAMILY_MOODS → arrange.js mood list (coarse half).
- ARCHETYPES energy → section planning, peak/mass ceiling (massMul), takeover guards.
- atlas vibe/speed/context features → neighborhood()/sectionOf()/pickFrom(), audition card tooltips.
